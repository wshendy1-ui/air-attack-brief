Data sources and limits
  • Incidents and perimeters: NIFC WFIGS, called live from this page. Perimeters lag the fire; each shows the date it was mapped.
  • Airport weather: METARs and TAFs from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, which mirrors National Weather Service data. Always confirm with an official briefing.
  • Airports and runways: OurAirports (public domain), filtered to US airports with a hard-surfaced runway of 4,000 ft or more. Runway headings are true, matching METAR wind directions.
  • Terrain elevation at the fire: USGS Elevation Point Query Service.
  • Replay mode: incident points and day-by-day perimeters come from the WFIGS year-to-date services. Acres follow the perimeter mapped on or before the replay time. Complexity level reflects the incident's latest record, and containment for that day is not available.
  • Not connected yet: TFRs, route weather advisories (G-AIRMET, SIGMET, CWA), and SUA/MTR geometry. NOTAMs, TFRs and military airspace are manual checks.
