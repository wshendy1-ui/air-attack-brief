Data sources and limits
  • Incidents and perimeters: NIFC WFIGS, called live from this page. Perimeters lag the fire; each shows the date it was mapped.
  • Airport weather: METARs and TAFs from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, which mirrors National Weather Service data. Always confirm with an official briefing.
  • Airports and runways: OurAirports (public domain), filtered to US airports with a hard-surfaced runway of 4,000 ft or more. Runway headings are true, matching METAR wind directions.
  • Terrain elevation at the fire: USGS Elevation Point Query Service.
  • Fire weather zones: outlines from the NOAA/NWS reference map service. Red Flag Warnings and Fire Weather Watches come from the NWS alerts feed in Live mode and the Iowa Environmental Mesonet warning archive in Replay. Start counts are every WFIGS wildfire discovered in the zone in the last 24 and 48 hours, including fires already out.
  • Flight times: the first 10 minutes after takeoff and the last 10 minutes before landing are figured at 70% of cruise speed to allow for climb and descent.
  • Replay mode: incident points and day-by-day perimeters come from the WFIGS year-to-date services. Acres follow the perimeter mapped on or before the replay time. Complexity level reflects the incident's latest record, and containment for that day is not available.
  • TFRs: shapes, altitudes and times from tfr.faa.gov, copied into this site by a scheduled job about every 20 minutes. The page shows the age of that copy and stops using it after an hour. Always confirm TFRs with an official source. Not available in Replay.
  • Route weather advisories: G-AIRMETs, SIGMETs and Center Weather Advisories from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet's copy of Aviation Weather Center products, checked against the route for the planned flight window. High-altitude turbulence and freezing-level G-AIRMETs are left out.
  • Military airspace: special use airspace and military training routes from the FAA's aeronautical data service, with published altitudes and times of use. Whether an area or route is active is not published in a form the page can read, so confirm with dispatch.
  • NOTAMs remain a manual check.
